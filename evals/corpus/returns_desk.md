Returns desk policy: customers may return unopened items within 30 days of delivery with a receipt. Opened electronics carry a 15 percent restocking fee unless the item is defective.

Store credit is issued when a receipt is missing. The credit equals the lowest selling price of the item during the previous 60 days.

Warehouse bin WH-22-B holds returned power tools awaiting inspection. Staff log the bin code on the return ticket before moving any item to resale stock.

Part number PX-4410-QZ is the replacement battery for the cordless drill line. Returns desk staff should quote PX-4410-QZ exactly when ordering a warranty replacement.
